Fun Facts: Fact 1: Jupiter is the biggest planet of the Solar System, with a mean radius of around 69.911 km, a diameter at the equator of about 142.984 km, and at the poles, the diameter is only around 133.708 km. Fact 2: Jupiter has a small axial tilt of around 3.13°. Fact 3: Jupiter has 3 ring systems though they are fainter and smaller than Saturn's. They are mostly made up of dust and small rocky pieces. Fact 4: A distinct feature of Jupiter is its Great Red Spot, a persistent high-pressure region in the atmosphere that produces an anticyclonic storm. Fact 5: Jupiter has a gravity of 24.79 m/s².
