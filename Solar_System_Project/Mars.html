Fun Facts: Fact 1: The gravity on Mars is about 38% of Earth's gravity. Fact 2: One rotation on Mars is completed within 24.6 hours while a whole trip around the Sun, is completed within 669.6 days. Fact 3: Mars' axis of rotation is tilted around 25.2°. Fact 4: Its atmosphere is mostly composed of carbon dioxide, nitrogen and argon gases. Fact 5: Mars' mass is 6.42 x 10^23 kilograms, about 10 times less than Earth's mass.
