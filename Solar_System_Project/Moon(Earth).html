Fun Facts: Fact 1: The moon's gravitational pull is responsible for Earth's low and high ocean tides. Fact 2: The moon's gravitational pull is around 1/6 of Earth's. Fact 3: The moon has eight phases: new moon, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full moon, waning gibbous, third quarter, and waning crescent. Fact 4: The dark spots of the moon is called maria, which are composed of solidified volcanic rocks, covering around 16% of the moon's surface. Fact 5: The moon is estimated to be around 4.5 billion years old.
