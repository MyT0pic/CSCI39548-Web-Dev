Fun Facts: Fact 1: Neptune's rotation is around 16 hours, while its revolution around the sun is around 165 Earth years. Fact 2: Neptune has a total of 6 known rings with some containing ring arcs or clusters of dust particles in a ring. Fact 3: Neptune is the only planet that's is invisible to the human eyes unless observed through a telescope. Fact 4: Neptune has the greatest density out of all the gas giants, at about 1.64 g/cm³. Fact 5: Neptune has the fastest wind speeds of any planet at around a wind speed of 2,160 km/h.
