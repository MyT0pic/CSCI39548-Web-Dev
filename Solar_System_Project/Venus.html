Fun Facts: Fact 1: Venus doesn't have any moons or ring systems and its magnetosphere is weak due to its slow rotation. Fact 2: It is the only planet named after a female deity and it is the brightest planet in the Solar System. Fact 3: Venus reflects around 70% of all the sunlight it receives. Fact 4: Venus completes its rotation, about every 243 Earth days, longer than its revolution around the sun which is around 225 Earth days. Fact 5: Venus has the least eccentric orbit, orbiting nearly in a perfect circle.
